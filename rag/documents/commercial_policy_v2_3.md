# Opella Global Commercial Policy & Pricing Framework (v2.3)

## 1. Executive Summary
This policy governs pricing discretion, mandatory rebate structures, and revenue recognition guidelines for all Opella commercial consumer healthcare brands across EMEA, APAC, and Americas.

## 2. Revenue Recognition Definitions
- **Gross Revenue**: Invoice value before prompt-payment discounts, distribution allowances, or volume rebates.
- **Net Sales**: Gross Revenue minus standard commercial deductions (trade discounts, returns provision, statutory rebates). Net Sales is the primary KPI for executive reporting and bonus threshold determination.
- **Discretionary Discounting Cap**: Local commercial leads may approve commercial discounts up to 8.5% without regional CFO authorization. Discounts exceeding 12% require central Global Commercial Committee (GCC) approval.

## 3. Brand Portfolio Classification
- **Core Megabrands**:
  - **Doliprane**: Analgesics category leader. Target gross margin: 68%. Mandatory safety stock: 45 days.
  - **Buscopan**: Antispasmodic GI category leader. Target gross margin: 72%.
  - **Allegra**: Allergy / Antihistamines. Seasonal surge buffer: 60 days pre-spring.
  - **Dulcolax**: Gastrointestinal / Laxatives.
  - **Mucosolvan**: Respiratory / Cough.

## 4. Compliance & Audit Disclosures
All financial figures reported in external filings or executive cockpit views must derive from the governed Snowflake analytical warehouse models (`marts.fct_sales_monthly`). Uncertified data sources are strictly prohibited in executive decision memos.
