# Opella Global Supply Chain Standard Operating Procedures (SOP-SC-401)

## 1. Inventory Thresholds & Days of Supply (DOS)
To prevent stockouts of life-essential and high-velocity consumer health products, regional distribution hubs must adhere to the following inventory policies:

| Product Class | Minimum Safety Stock | Reorder Trigger (DOS) | Critical Shortage Alert |
|---|---|---|---|
| Critical Analgesics (Doliprane) | 35 days | 45 days | < 21 days |
| Antihistamines (Allegra - In Season) | 40 days | 60 days | < 25 days |
| GI Care (Buscopan, Dulcolax) | 30 days | 40 days | < 18 days |
| Respiratory (Mucosolvan) | 30 days | 45 days | < 20 days |

## 2. Automated Escalation & Incident Management
When Days of Supply (DOS) falls below Critical Shortage Alert thresholds:
1. The AI Decision Cockpit automatically generates a **Priority-2 Incident in ServiceNow**.
2. An expedited stock redistribution recommendation is generated from regional backup hubs (e.g., Lyon Hub to Central Distribution).
3. Commercial leads are notified to throttle discretionary promotional activities in the affected geography until inventory levels stabilize above 35 days.
